Experiment settings and limitations

Playback reads recorded Python results. It does not rerun or retune the filters. Both scenarios share the same sensor-noise realizations. Plot scales stay fixed between scenarios.

Accelerometer tilt assumes gravity dominates the measurement. Translation can violate that assumption. This single-axis simulation does not establish accuracy on a physical drone, or embedded or flight performance.

Source: meridian.ekf_experiment, nominal and translation-pulse exports. The download includes full-run metrics and source-file SHA-256 fingerprints.
