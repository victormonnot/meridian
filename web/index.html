Experiment settings and limitations

Playback reads recorded Python results. It does not rerun or retune the filters. The nominal/translation pair shares sensor-noise realizations. Controlled cases reuse noise draws by sample order; motion timing, bias, noise scale and acquisition delay can change the raw measurements. Scales cover all methods, including hidden curves; they may change when selecting another scenario.

Accelerometer tilt assumes gravity dominates the measurement. Translation can violate that assumption. This single-axis simulation does not establish accuracy on a physical drone, or embedded or flight performance.
